ARDUINIUM — animated background
Brief for the hero (/) and registration (/register) backgrounds.

Implemented in app/globals.css as `.ard-gradient`, wrapped by
components/site/gradient-bg.tsx. This file is the intent behind it; the CSS is
the implementation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

A warm, slow, ambient field of colour behind the hero copy. Four soft lamps
drifting across a cream page — as if light were falling across a workbench,
not as if a SaaS template had a gradient applied to it.

It sits UNDER the interactive perfboard grid. The gradient is soft colour with
no edges; the grid is small hard dots. Layering them reads as a lit circuit
board. Two effects of the same visual weight would fight.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PALETTE — no colour outside this list
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  base      #F8F1DE   cream page, always the ground
  orange    #D9612F   power / LED motif, at ~34% opacity
  teal      #1E8F82   signal / circuit motif, at ~32% opacity
  navy-2    #28495F   depth, at ~22% opacity, anchored LOW

Explicitly forbidden: purple, magenta, indigo, electric blue, any two-stop
"vibrant" linear gradient, any mesh-gradient generator output. Those are the
visual signature of a generic AI-generated SaaS page and the product's whole
claim is that it is not one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each lamp drifts and scales on its own long cycle:

  orange   19s
  teal     23s
  navy-2   27s

The three periods are mutually prime, so the composition never visibly
repeats — no moment where a viewer recognises "it just looped".

  - ease-in-out, alternating direction
  - translate + scale ONLY. No background-position, no filter animation, no
    hue rotation. Transform stays on the compositor; the others force paint
    every frame and will drop the hero to single-digit FPS on a school laptop,
    which is precisely the machine this has to run on.
  - blur(58px) applied once, statically, never animated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONTRAST — non-negotiable
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The headline sits on top of this. Peak opacity of any lamp is capped so that
navy #1C3547 text over the busiest point of the animation still clears
WCAG AA. The navy lamp is anchored to the BOTTOM of the hero for this reason:
it is the darkest field and the headline is at the top.

If a colour has to get louder to look good, the answer is a bigger, softer,
more transparent shape — not a more saturated one.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) stops the drift and keeps the
composition. The page still looks designed; it does not fall back to a flat
rectangle. Nothing about the layout depends on the animation running.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHERE IT APPEARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  /            hero section only, behind the ripple grid
  /register    full section, no grid over it — the role cards and the form are
               the focus there, and a second interactive layer under a form is
               a distraction rather than an invitation.

Nowhere else. It is the arrival moment; using it on every section would make
it wallpaper.
